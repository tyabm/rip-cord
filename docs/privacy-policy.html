Rip-Cord Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Rip-Cord is a personal habit-interruption app. It is designed so that core features work entirely on your device. This policy describes what the app stores and what it does not.

Who we are

Rip-Cord is published as a personal / independent Android application (package com.ripcord.rip_cord). It is a habit and attention tool, not a medical device and not a clinical service.

What we collect

Rip-Cord does not require an account and does not operate a cloud backend for core features. We do not sell personal information.

The app stores the following only on your phone:

  • Competing-response text you use or edit
  • Activation log (time, success/abort, duration, points, source)
  • Settings (theme, duration, hand-off choice, optional music link, optional custom color)
  • Points and streak values
  • An optional trusted person's name and phone number, if you type them in. Rip-Cord does not read your contacts. The number stays on this device and is used only to open your SMS app or dialer. Rip-Cord does not send messages, place calls, or track replies

This data stays in app-private storage. Clearing app data or using “Clear all data” in Settings deletes it.

What we do not do

  • No login, advertising ID collection, or analytics SDK for core features
  • No cloud sync of your log or responses
  • No sale or rental of user data
  • No tracking of other apps or phone unlocks
  • No reading of contacts, and no sending of SMS on your behalf

Optional on-device features

  • Text-to-speech: spoken prompts stay on the device via the system TTS engine.
  • CSV export / share: you choose when to export. Sharing uses the Android share sheet you pick (email, Files, etc.).
  • Breathing chime: a sound file bundled with the app; playback is local.
  • Widgets and Quick Settings: used only to launch Rip-Cord on your device.

Some libraries may declare an Internet permission even though Rip-Cord does not send your habit data to our servers. We do not use that permission to upload your log or settings.

Children

Rip-Cord is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete competing responses in Settings
  • Export your log as CSV
  • Clear all data in Settings, or uninstall the app

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

For privacy questions about Rip-Cord, use the email address listed on the Google Play store listing for this app.
